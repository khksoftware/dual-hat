# Dual Hat 1.9.0 Release Notes

Dual Hat 1.9.0 adds three proportional lifecycle controls.

Forward-looking artifacts—including roadmaps, backlogs, technical debt, and
future-work triggers—are groomed at defined progression points and material
changes. Live items advance or retain an explicit review route; obsolete,
duplicate, satisfied, and stale entries are completed, retired, or refreshed.

Closure now reconciles promised stakeholder-facing deliverables. A report,
comparison, recommendation, or decision summary is not considered delivered
merely because its source artifact was committed or archived; it must be
presented proactively or explicitly reported as deferred or blocked.

Research, experimentation, tuning, and similar repeated work normally remain
explicit iterations inside one capability when the objective, authority,
product boundary, rollback unit, and acceptance contract remain unchanged. A
new capability is reserved for a material contract change, not attempt-number
churn. Fresh sealed evidence may still be required for later iterations.

This is a backward-compatible additive minor release. Existing 1.x interfaces,
historical work items, authority boundaries, and mandatory safeguards remain
valid.
