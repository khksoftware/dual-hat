# Dual Hat 1.17.0 Release Notes

Dual Hat 1.17.0 adds lightweight work routing, sharper role composition and
diagnosis, safer shared-artifact parallelism, durable-learning hygiene, and a
mandatory turn-exit audit without changing schemas or weakening any 1.x
contract.

Discover, decide, deliver, and single-role pass are now optional descriptions
of an activity's intended end, not a mandatory pipeline. Work uses the smallest
role roster that adds distinct value against its actual failure axes. When an
agent struggles, re-tiering addresses insufficient model/runtime capability,
while re-roling addresses an authority, method, or judgment mismatch.
Role transitions now distinguish re-tiering, primary-hat transition, and
specialist reassignment at their respective safe boundaries. A single-role pass
never bypasses Architecture acceptance or archival authority.

During parallel or shared mutation, every shared artifact lane has one active
writer at a time and one integration owner. Trivial serial work uses implicit
primary ownership; reassignment requires a quiescent checkpoint and
partial-state handoff. Independent reviewers stay read-only on that lane and
return isolated findings for central reconciliation. At a genuinely governed
blocked boundary, Engineering either delivers the bounded outcome or compactly
declares the attempted work, exact obstacle, needed capability or decision,
preserved state, and recommended escalation; recoverable failures and
milestones remain nonterminal. Blocked work stays unaccepted and unarchived
until its recorded re-entry condition is satisfied.

The Architecture and Engineering guides now require a turn-exit audit before
every response boundary. A status report, aside, correction, or governance
update cannot terminate active execution, and a forced host yield carries a
resumable next-action receipt and continuation mechanism.

The carried-forward continuity controls are now one coherent group:
termination preflight, the active-goal response/continuation interlock, the
cross-response execution lease, and the response-end worker watchdog.
Validation and transition integrity likewise groups separation of validation
gates from authorized mutations, nonzero-test execution evidence,
lifecycle-aware gate inputs, and pre-state-versus-replay transition gates.
Itemized adjudication readiness, identity-bound progress accounting, and
current-capability claim verification close the corresponding completeness and
evidence gaps.

Durable learning is retained only when reusable or materially control-improving;
there is no mandatory per-run lesson ledger. Accumulated framework releases and
governed phase progressions consolidate duplicates, resolve contradictions,
review staleness and scope, and promote cross-context guidance only from
supporting evidence.

Execution-ready plans now receive a proportionate optimization pass for
brute-force avoidance, value-first ordering, dependency sequence,
parallelism/resources, incremental checkpoints, evidence reuse, and cheaper
equivalent controls. A sealed independent Architecture optimization review is
reserved for scale, complexity, risk, or irreversibility that warrants it.
Long-running or materially resource-consuming execution is reevaluated only at
proportionate meaningful checkpoints for changed bottlenecks, throughput,
failures, value yield, allocation, batching, cost, and wall time; healthy work
does not pause for a ceremonial review.
Material assumptions and hypotheses embedded in an active design or plan are
retested against current evidence and explicitly confirmed, revised, or
retired. Unchallenged assumptions are not reported as supported, and
experimentation remains proportional to consequence and uncertainty.

Portable-core source remains free of plugin deployment artifacts. A standalone
distribution may own disjoint plugin manifests, wrappers, installation
projections, packages, release notes, and deployment provenance; forward core
publication preserves those namespaces without treating them as core drift.
The portable changelog remains byte-exact canonical content and does not merge
standalone-only deployment history.

Shipped validation, test, staging, packaging, planning, and bootstrap
entrypoints now suppress bytecode generation before loading local tooling.
Governed staging also removes and reports only contained, recognized
`__pycache__`, `.pyc`, and `.pyo` artifacts before its manifest mismatch gate;
unrecognized files remain untouched and fail closed.

Those three carried-forward groups plus the outcome-routing/role/learning
changes and the plan/run optimization changes account for all 13 items carried
into the unpublished portable 1.17.0 candidate.

This is a backward-compatible additive governance release. Existing 1.x
authority, lifecycle, schema, profile, handover, and compatibility contracts
remain unchanged.
